# Pinstripe Prep

A Yankees-themed ACT English practice game. Every question is a rematch of
one Levi missed on a practice test: new sentence, same rule, same trap.

**Play:** https://ldm-baller.github.io/pinstripe-prep/

## How it works

- **Play Ball:** a 3-inning game against a random rival, 3 at-bats an inning.
  **The Full Nine** is 27 at-bats against the Red Sox.
- **Right answer = a hit.** Beat the 42-second pitch clock (real ACT pace) for
  extra bases: under 15 seconds is a home run. Three in a row and you're on fire,
  so every hit goes one base farther.
- **Wrong answer = strikeout.** The app flags the trap choice, explains why it's
  wrong, and sends the question to the **Film Room**. It keeps coming back until
  you hit it.
- **Batting Practice:** drill a single rule with no score.
- **Scouting Report:** your average on each rule, next to the question you
  missed on the real test.
- Take the lead in the bottom of the last inning and it's a **walk-off**.

On an iPhone, open the link in Safari and tap Share, then Add to Home Screen.

Rules covered so far: subject-verb agreement, pronoun agreement, tone and
register, transitions, relative pronouns, and paired punctuation around an
interruption.

The questions are original and written for this app. No ACT test material is
reproduced. Not affiliated with the New York Yankees or ACT, Inc.
